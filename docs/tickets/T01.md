# T01: Conversion uplift for the board slide

**From:** Pak Danu, Head of Growth, Tuesday 09:14
**Request:** "Bro, tolong dong angka konversi eksperimen kemarin. Buat slide direksi besok pagi. Yang penting persen naiknya berapa. Thanks!"

## The real question

Pak Danu needs one headline number for tomorrow's board slide. The risk is that the board reads "+15.5% conversion" as "roll it out", while the full analysis points the other way. The real question is: what can he put on the slide that is true, simple, and does not set up a decision the economics do not support?

## Assumptions I made

- "Persen naiknya" means the relative lift in conversion, variant B over variant A, which is the figure growth teams usually quote.
- Conversion = an order within 7 days of first exposure, measured on the cleaned population of 66,570 users (exposures outside the test window, bots, users who saw both variants, and repeat exposures removed).
- He has no time for the full analysis tonight, so the reply has to fit in one chat message.

## Answer

> Pak Danu, conversion went from **5.89% to 6.81%: +15.5%** (statistically solid, p < 0.001).
>
> One line I'd keep on the same slide: at today's shipping cost (we pay about Rp12k per free-shipping order), a full rollout returns roughly **Rp255M less contribution per year** than not running the promo. So the safest framing for the board is: *"The offer lifts conversion; the cost design needs one more iteration before rollout."*
>
> Happy to send a three-line companion slide tonight if useful.

## Limitations

- +15.5% is the six-week average. Variant B's conversion fell from 7.53% in weeks 1-2 to 6.11% in weeks 5-6, so part of the lift is a novelty effect and the long-run lift is likely smaller.
- Most free-shipping orders were not created by the offer. Per 1,000 users, variant B placed about 68 orders against 59 in variant A, so roughly 9 of every 68 were extra; the rest would likely have happened anyway.
- The split was skewed by a bucketing bug on Android build 6.4.0 (62.9% of its users went to B). The lift is statistically clear on Android, iOS and mobile web (+0.91pp to +1.25pp) and not detectable on desktop (+0.08pp, within noise), so the direction is reliable; the exact size is less certain.
- 95% confidence interval of the absolute difference: +0.55pp to +1.29pp.
- Rp255M rests on a Rp12,000 shipping-cost proxy, not the actual carrier rate.
