# T03: "Is WH-CKR's OTIF really that bad?"

**From:** Pak Yusuf, Head of Supply Chain, Thursday 11:02
**Request:** "Saya dengar kamu bilang OTIF WH-CKR jelek. Gudang saya nggak berubah apa-apa bulan Mei. Datanya dari mana? Jangan sampai salah, ini soal KPI tim saya."

## The real question

Pak Yusuf is asking two things: is the number correct, and is his team being blamed for it? He is right that the warehouse process did not change. What changed was the load on it. The answer has to confirm the number, show where it comes from, and move the explanation from "team performance" to "what happened around the team", without pointing at another department.

## Assumptions I made

- OTIF = delivered on or before the promised date **and** shipped complete. Parcels that were delivered but never scanned (1,672 overall) are excluded, not counted as failures.
- "May" = shipments that left the warehouse in May.
- He will want to check the data himself, so I offer the breakdown, not just the conclusion.

## Answer

> Pak Yusuf, thank you for checking. You are right that the team's process did not change, and the data supports that.
>
> **The number:** WH-CKR OTIF was 84-86% every month from January to April, 68.8% in May, and 80.1% in June. Source: TokoRia shipment scans (ship date, promised date, delivered date, fulfilment type).
>
> **What the data shows about the drop:**
>
> - **It is almost all lateness.** Of 1,806 May shipments that missed OTIF, 1,615 were late but complete. Incomplete shipments stayed at 3.3%, in line with March (3.0%) and April (3.3%).
> - **It hit every order equally.** Between 18 May and 3 June, 49% of orders without the promoted SKUs arrived late, versus 47% of orders with them. That is the pattern of a warehouse under load, not of specific products failing.
> - **The load rose sharply.** WH-CKR shipped about 955 parcels a week before the promo and 1,312-1,554 a week during it, 37-63% more.
> - **Replenishment for both hero SKUs did not arrive on 17 or 24 May.** The last delivery was 10 May and the next 31 May. Both SKUs were at zero stock from 22/23 to 30 May.
> - Over the same window, WH-BDG and WH-TGR stayed at 11-12% late.
>
> So I would describe May as a capacity and replenishment event triggered by the promo, not a drop in team performance, and I would like to note that on the KPI report.
>
> Two things the data cannot tell me, where your team's knowledge matters: why the 17 and 24 May replenishments did not arrive, and whether orders were held waiting for stock or queued for picking. Could we take 20 minutes on this? I would also like to propose an early-warning alert: days of cover for KRK-BLD-080 fell below 7 on 17 May, six days before it ran out.

## Limitations

- The data shows when and where the delays happened, not why. The cause inside the warehouse (picking backlog, held orders, staffing) needs the team's input.
- 1,672 parcels were never scanned on delivery. If they were mostly on time or mostly late, May's figure would move slightly.
- I do not know who owns the replenishment decision for 17 and 24 May, and the reply deliberately does not assign it.
