# T02: May GMV, dashboard vs TokoRia report

**From:** Bu Ratna, Finance, Wednesday 16:40
**Request:** "Angka GMV Mei di dashboard kamu Rp X, tapi laporan dari TokoRia Rp Y. Bedanya lumayan. Yang benar yang mana? Saya harus tutup buku Jumat."

## The real question

Bu Ratna is not really asking which system is right. She needs one number she can book on Friday, and confidence that it will not move afterwards. Neither figure is that number: both are gross sales, and GMV is a growth metric, not booked revenue.

## Assumptions I made

- The TokoRia figure was produced from the same raw export our pipeline receives, summed directly without cleaning. On that basis it comes to **Rp554,804,600** for May. Our dashboard shows **Rp552,336,200**.
- May = orders placed 1-31 May, Western Indonesia Time (WIB).
- For booking, revenue = completed orders only, each counted once, net of returns. The final booking rule is Finance's decision; this is my proposal.

## Answer

> Bu Ratna, the gap is **Rp2.47M (0.45%)**, and it has two causes that pull in opposite directions:
>
> 1. **Orders counted twice (+Rp3.86M on TokoRia's side).** When a payment webhook fires twice, the raw export records the same order twice. 69 May orders were affected. Our dashboard counts each order once.
> 2. **Returns (-Rp1.57M on TokoRia's side).** May orders had 66 return lines (192 units), and the raw export subtracts them from sales. Our dashboard reports sales before returns.
>
> A third, minor cause (+Rp0.18M on TokoRia's side): 6 orders placed between midnight and 4 a.m. on 1 June carry a 31 May date in the raw file, because their timestamps are stored in UTC.
>
> **For the books, I would use neither figure.** Both include orders that never became revenue: Rp22.46M cancelled and Rp6.59M refunded. The figure I would book for May is **Rp521,719,000**: completed orders, each counted once, net of returns.
>
> To stop this recurring, I need two things: engineering to make the payment webhook idempotent so duplicates never reach the export, and 15 minutes with you to agree one definition of net revenue, which I will add to the dashboard next to GMV.

## Limitations

- If TokoRia's actual figure is not Rp554.8M, there is a cause I have not found. I would need their May breakdown by order status to close it.
- The data has no return date, so returns are attributed to the month of the original order. If Finance books returns in the month they happen, the timing will shift slightly.
- Refunds are assumed to be for the full order value.
- The dashboard's Total GMV includes cancelled and refunded orders by design, because it is GMV. The card label needs to say so.
